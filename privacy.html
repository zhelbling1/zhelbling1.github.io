Privacy Policy

App: rclone — FTDATACOLLECT1 Drive uploader
Last updated: November 16, 2026

Who uses this app

This app is a private tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to or used by the public.

What Google data it accesses

The app requests only the Google Drive drive.file permission. With it, the app can create files in the owner's Google Drive and see or manage only those files. It cannot access any other files, emails, contacts, or other Google account data.

How the data is used

The app copies monitoring data files recorded on one field computer (FTDATACOLLECT1) into a folder in the owner's Google Drive, once a day. That is its only purpose.

Storage and sharing

  • Uploaded files are stored only in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • The sign-in token that lets the app upload is stored only on that field computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with, or sent to any third party, and nothing is used for advertising or analytics.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uploaded files remain in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them.

Google API Services User Data Policy

This app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
